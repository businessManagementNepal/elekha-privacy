eLekha Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

eLekha (“eLekha,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides an offline-first business management application designed to help users manage business and financial records on their own devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information eLekha handles, how that information is used and stored, the choices available to users, and how users can delete their information.

Offline-first notice: eLekha V1 stores profile and business information locally on the user's device. eLekha V1 does not automatically upload business records to eLekha-operated servers and does not provide cloud synchronization or automatic online backup.

1. Offline-First Design

The current version of eLekha is designed to operate locally on the user's device. Profile, business, accounting, inventory, transaction, and related application data are stored locally on the device.

eLekha V1 does not automatically upload this locally stored business data to eLekha-operated servers.

If eLekha introduces optional cloud services, subscriptions, cloud backup, or multi-device synchronization in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those services are used to process user data.

2. Information Handled by eLekha

Profile and Authentication Information

Depending on the information you provide, eLekha may store locally:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, if provided
  • Login identifier
  • Local user/profile information
  • Local account and workspace information
  • Authentication credentials

Passwords are processed locally. eLekha is designed to store a password hash rather than the user's plaintext password.

Business and Financial Information

Information entered into eLekha may include:

  • Business profile information
  • Products and product categories
  • Inventory and inventory movements
  • Customers, suppliers, and other contacts
  • Sales and point-of-sale records
  • Orders
  • Invoices and billing records
  • Transactions and expenses
  • Money accounts
  • Ledger entries
  • Budgets
  • EMI and loan tracking records
  • Notes and reports
  • Tax and regional finance settings
  • Staff and local user-management information
  • Other business records entered by the user

Files and Information Selected by the User

When supported by a feature, eLekha may access files, documents, images, or other content that the user intentionally selects for importing, attaching, displaying, exporting, or sharing.

Application Information

eLekha may store application information locally, including:

  • Selected language
  • Appearance preferences
  • Active profile and account selection
  • Application settings
  • Import history or local audit records
  • Locally generated reminder and notification information
  • Application version and device platform information used for app functionality or support

3. How Information Is Used

Information stored in eLekha is used to provide app functionality, including:

  • Authenticating the local user
  • Managing businesses and workspaces
  • Managing products and inventory
  • Recording sales, expenses, orders, and transactions
  • Creating invoices and billing records
  • Maintaining ledgers and money accounts
  • Tracking budgets, EMI, and loans
  • Managing contacts
  • Producing reports and calculations
  • Importing and exporting user data
  • Managing local users and permissions
  • Saving user preferences
  • Providing local reminders and notifications

eLekha does not use locally stored business data for targeted advertising.

4. Storage of Information

The current offline version of eLekha stores application and business data primarily within the app's local storage and local database on the user's device.

Because this version does not provide eLekha cloud backup, eLekha generally cannot remotely view, restore, recover, modify, or delete information that exists only on the user's device.

Users are responsible for maintaining any exported copies they wish to preserve.

5. Device Permissions

Camera or Image Access

Used when a user chooses to capture or select an image for an eLekha feature.

Files and Documents

Used when the user chooses to import, export, open, create, or share business documents or data files.

Notifications

Used for local reminders and notifications generated by eLekha.

Users can control supported permissions through their device settings. The production version of eLekha V1 does not require microphone recording functionality.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

eLekha does not sell or rent users' locally stored business data.

The offline version does not automatically share locally stored profile or business records with eLekha-operated servers.

Information may leave the device when the user intentionally:

  • Exports business data
  • Shares a document or report
  • Sends information through another application
  • Sends an email to eLekha support
  • Opens an external website or service

When information is intentionally sent to another application, email provider, website, cloud-storage provider, or other third party, that third party's privacy practices may apply.

eLekha cannot control copies of information that the user has exported or shared outside the eLekha application.

7. Support Communications

If a user contacts eLekha at support@e-lekha.com, eLekha may receive the information the user voluntarily includes in the communication, such as an email address, description of an issue, screenshots, or other information.

Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain appropriate support records, resolve disputes, protect the service, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive business or financial information in support communications.

8. Data Retention

Information stored only within eLekha on the user's device remains there until it is:

  • Deleted by the user
  • Removed using eLekha's Delete Profile & All Data feature
  • Removed when application storage is cleared
  • Otherwise removed from the device

eLekha does not maintain a cloud copy of offline V1 business data for automatic restoration.

Files that a user has exported, downloaded, shared, copied, or stored outside eLekha are not deleted when the eLekha local database is deleted.

9. Delete Profile & All Data

Users can permanently delete their local eLekha profile and all eLekha application data stored by the app on the device.

The deletion option is available from eLekha's Data & Privacy settings.

Deleting the profile and all data removes locally stored eLekha information, including, as applicable:

  • User/profile records
  • Authentication credentials
  • Businesses and workspaces
  • Products and categories
  • Inventory
  • Contacts
  • Transactions and expenses
  • Orders and POS records
  • Invoices and billing records
  • Ledgers
  • Money accounts
  • Budgets
  • EMI and loan records
  • Notes
  • Local users and permissions
  • Application settings
  • Local audit and import records
  • Locally stored synchronization metadata
  • Local session information
  • Other records stored in the eLekha local database

The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Users should export any information they wish to retain before deleting their profile and data.

Deleting eLekha data does not delete files or records that the user previously exported or shared with another application or service.

Because eLekha V1 operates locally, eLekha support generally cannot remotely recover deleted information.

10. Your Data Choices and Rights

Access

Users can access their locally stored information directly within the eLekha application. Where supported, users can use Export Data to create a copy of their business information.

Correction

Users can correct or update profile, business, financial, contact, inventory, and other editable information directly within eLekha.

Portability

eLekha provides export functionality for supported data and file formats. Available export formats and modules may depend on the version of the application.

Deletion

Users can use Delete Profile & All Data to permanently remove their eLekha profile and all eLekha application data stored locally on the device.

Device Permissions

Users may grant or revoke supported device permissions through Android or other device settings.

Privacy Questions

Users may contact support@e-lekha.com for questions about this Privacy Policy or eLekha's privacy practices. Because offline V1 data is stored on the user's own device, support may not have access to data that has never been transmitted to eLekha.

11. Security

eLekha uses reasonable technical measures appropriate to an offline application to help protect locally stored information.

Authentication credentials are designed to be stored using password hashing rather than plaintext passwords.

Application data is stored within the application's local storage environment and is subject to the security protections of the user's device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. Users should protect their devices with appropriate device security, keep their operating systems updated, and export important records when appropriate.

12. Children's Privacy

eLekha is a business and financial record-management application and is not designed or directed specifically toward children.

Users should comply with applicable laws when entering information relating to other individuals into the application.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

eLekha may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality or privacy practices change.

Material changes involving cloud services, remote accounts, subscriptions, synchronization, or new forms of data processing will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy.

The effective date at the top of this policy indicates when the policy was last updated.
