# eLekha Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the public Privacy Policy for **eLekha**, an offline-first business management application.

## Privacy Policy

The live Privacy Policy is published using GitHub Pages.

**Privacy Policy URL:**  
https://businessmanagementnepal.github.io/elekha-privacy/

## About eLekha

eLekha helps users manage business records such as:

- Products and inventory
- Sales and POS
- Expenses and transactions
- Orders and invoices
- Ledger and money accounts
- Contacts
- Budgets
- EMI and loan records
- Reports

The current V1 release is designed as a local-first application. Business data is primarily stored on the user's device.
